Bibliography {#biblio}
============

Works the documentation cites or relies on. The [BibTeX file](polyfloat.bibtex) holds the same
references.

# Floating-point expansions

  + Y. Hida, X. S. Li, D. H. Bailey, *Library for Double-Double and Quad-Double Arithmetic*,
    2008. [Free copy](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/qd.pdf).
    The double-double and quad-double numbers, unevaluated sums of two and four `double`.

  + M. Joldes, O. Marty, J.-M. Muller, V. Popescu, *Arithmetic Algorithms for Extended Precision
    Using Floating-Point Expansions*, IEEE Transactions on Computers **65** (4), 2016,
    pp. 1197-1210. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TC.2015.2441714),
    [free copy](https://hal.science/hal-01111551v2/document).
    The normalization and the arithmetic of expansions of any length.

  + J. van der Hoeven, *Multiple Precision Floating-Point Arithmetic on SIMD Processors*, IEEE
    Symposium on Computer Arithmetic (ARITH), 2017, pp. 2-9.
    [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ARITH.2017.12), [free copy](https://hal.science/hal-01429594).
    Expansions computed on SIMD registers.

  + D. K. Zhang, A. Aiken, *High-Performance Branch-Free Algorithms for Extended-Precision
    Floating-Point Arithmetic*, International Conference for High Performance Computing,
    Networking, Storage and Analysis (SC), 2025, pp. 695-710.
    [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3712285.3759876).
    Arithmetic on expansions without branches.

  + D. K. Zhang, *Fast Algorithms for High-Precision Floating-Point Arithmetic*, Stanford
    Digital Repository, 2025. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.25740/gt930wy1453).

# Floating-point arithmetic

  + J.-M. Muller, N. Brunie, F. de Dinechin, C.-P. Jeannerod, M. Joldes, V. Lefèvre,
    G. Melquiond, N. Revol, S. Torres, *Handbook of Floating-Point Arithmetic*, 2nd edition,
    Birkhäuser, 2018. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-76526-6).
    The error-free transformations of a sum and of a product.

  + S. Boldo, C.-P. Jeannerod, G. Melquiond, J.-M. Muller, *Floating-Point Arithmetic*, Acta
    Numerica **32**, 2023, pp. 203-290. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0962492922000101).

  + S. Graillat, J.-M. Muller, *Emulation of the FMA and the Correctly-Rounded Sum of Three
    Numbers in Rounding-to-Nearest Floating-Point Arithmetic*, Numerische Mathematik, 2025.
    [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00211-025-01487-2),
    [free copy](https://hal.science/hal-04575249).
