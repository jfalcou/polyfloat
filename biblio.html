Floating-point expansions

  • Y. Hida, X. S. Li, D. H. Bailey, Library for Double-Double and Quad-Double Arithmetic,
    1. Free copy. The double-double and quad-double numbers, unevaluated sums of two and four double.
  • M. Joldes, O. Marty, J.-M. Muller, V. Popescu, Arithmetic Algorithms for Extended Precision Using Floating-Point Expansions, IEEE Transactions on Computers 65 (4), 2016, pp. 1197-1210. DOI, free copy. The normalization and the arithmetic of expansions of any length.
  • J. van der Hoeven, Multiple Precision Floating-Point Arithmetic on SIMD Processors, IEEE Symposium on Computer Arithmetic (ARITH), 2017, pp. 2-9. DOI, free copy. Expansions computed on SIMD registers.
  • D. K. Zhang, A. Aiken, High-Performance Branch-Free Algorithms for Extended-Precision Floating-Point Arithmetic, International Conference for High Performance Computing, Networking, Storage and Analysis (SC), 2025, pp. 695-710. DOI. Arithmetic on expansions without branches.
  • D. K. Zhang, Fast Algorithms for High-Precision Floating-Point Arithmetic, Stanford Digital Repository, 2025. DOI.

Floating-point arithmetic
